APA Corporation Stock Pitch — GFA Competition

Investment Thesis

BUY with $44 base case target (+84% upside)

APA Corporation is intrinsically undervalued relative to peers and historical multiples despite strong fundamentals:

  • Revenue doubled from COVID lows while P/B and P/S remain at pandemic levels
  • Diversified commodity exposure: 56% oil, 35% natural gas, 9% NGLs
  • Strategic M&A (Callon Petroleum acquisition) increased Permian Basin production 25%
